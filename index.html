The studio

A studio of one, building like it matters.

Level X is an independent app studio. No growth-at-all-costs playbook, no dark patterns, no selling attention back to families. Just small, deliberate apps made by someone who actually uses them.

Every app starts from a real moment in family life — a tense morning, a habit that won't stick, a conversation that keeps getting put off — and tries to make it a little softer. If a feature doesn't earn its place, it doesn't ship.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is Level X?
Level X is a small independent app studio that builds calm, careful mobile apps for kids and families. Its first app is Pausely, a mindfulness app, with Prevail and OffMyMind in development.
Are your apps safe for kids?
Yes. Level X designs for families first. Apps are built to be privacy-first and follow Google Play Families and COPPA guidance — no surprise data collection, no advertising aimed at children, and nothing sold to third parties.
What is Pausely?
Pausely is a mindfulness app for kids and families. It offers short, guided moments to pause, breathe, and reset together during a busy day — gentle enough for young children and useful for parents too.
When are Prevail and OffMyMind launching?
Both are in active development. Prevail focuses on forgiving habit-building, and OffMyMind is a space for couples to stay connected. Email us to hear when they're ready.
