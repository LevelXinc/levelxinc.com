    Before you book a call

    The things people ask first. If yours isn't here, bring it to the call.

    What does Level X actually do?

    Level X designs and builds websites for small and mid-sized businesses, and then builds the system underneath the website: booking, intake, follow-up and reminders. The work starts by mapping the path a customer takes from first contact to a booked, paid job and finding the points where people drop out, whether that is slow replies, a heavy booking form, unanswered calls, no follow-up or no-shows. That includes automation and AI where it genuinely helps.

    Does Level X build websites from scratch?

    Yes. Level X builds new websites from scratch, and upgrades existing ones. Website builds start at $999, and the price is agreed before any work begins. A business with no website at all is fine as long as it already has a Google listing and is getting inquiries, because that means there is a real customer path to build around.

    Who is Level X for?

    Service and retail businesses in the United States that already get inquiries and want more of them to turn into booked work. Level X starts with one fixed-price sprint on a single problem, so you get to see the work and the results before deciding on anything longer.

    What is the free AI check?

    Enter a website address and Level X runs an automated review of that site's customer path, then reports the likely leaks. Each website gets one free check. Run one on yours.

    How does an engagement work?

    It starts with a short conversation, then a customer path audit, then a fixed-scope sprint to put the fixes in place. Scope and price are agreed before any work begins, so there is no open-ended bill.

    Does Level X use AI in its own work?

    Level X started with a mom trying to juggle homeschooling her kids and running a business from the same kitchen table. The first automations were small, like checking homework and keeping the day from falling apart, and they gave back hours that had to come from somewhere. That is still how Level X sees AI and automation. They are tools that help people do their work better, not replacements for the people doing it. Every one gets guardrails: it says what it is, it hands off to a person when it should, and it never pretends to be one. The tools on this website are running examples, so you can see what they do and where they stop.

    Do you build apps too?

    Yes. Level X builds its own mobile apps, including Pausely, a mindfulness app for kids and families that is live on Google Play. See the apps.
