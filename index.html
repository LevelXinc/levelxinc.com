    What a fix looks like

    Most of what we fix is pretty ordinary, like a booking page that breaks on a phone or a voicemail box nobody checks. Ordinary problems still cost real customers.

    What runs in the background

    Once booking works, these are the pieces we usually set up so nobody has to remember to follow up.

    • New inquiries get sortedEach one is logged and sent to the right person on your team.
    • Reminders go out by textCustomers can confirm with a one-letter reply, which cuts down on no-shows.
    • Your customers live in one listYou can see who called, who booked and who hasn't been back in a while.
